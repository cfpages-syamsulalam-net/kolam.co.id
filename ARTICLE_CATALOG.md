## Catalog rules

- This is a planning catalog, not permission to publish every row.
- One row owns one primary user intent.
- `Coverage` states what the article must answer.
- `Boundary / links` states what it must not duplicate and names its natural cluster links.
- Slugs are proposed canonical slugs; verify against the live index and redirects before use.
- Technical, safety, chemical, electrical, structural, public-health, and standards content requires current primary sources and qualified review.
- Existing content must be consolidated before a proposed article receives production status.

Status legend: `🆕` proposed, `♻️` rewrite/merge target, `🧪` tool/evidence asset, `⛔` do not create without distinct evidence.
